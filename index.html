01 / BACKGROUND

Curiosity, backed
by hands-on practice.

I am developing my foundation in cybersecurity with a particular interest in Security Operations, threat detection and digital forensics.

I enjoy learning by working through practical scenarios: examining suspicious activity, understanding what logs can reveal, exploring security tools and recording what the evidence tells me. I am especially interested in the investigative thinking behind a SOC workflow — from an initial alert or report to analysis, a reasoned conclusion and clear documentation.

This portfolio is a working record of that process. It will grow as I complete projects, practise in controlled labs and turn new knowledge into evidence I can explain.
